**Day1**

This day was focused on introducing the basics of version control via GitHub. This is essential to know what version control looks like and why it is important. For projects with multiple contributors, dividing tasks can be challenging. The concepts of fork and branch help us to manage a comprehensive project at scale with a safe and robust structure. Today, an interesting concept discussed was conflict management. There is always a risk that contributions from different developers may not align. Version control tools assist us in addressing these issues and allow us to verify the project step by step.

**Day2**

Today, the essentials of working on a local repository were covered. git commands provide a comprehensive set of tools (show, grep, graph, bisect, annotate, and ...) to work on a project through the terminal. The strength of these tools lies in their ability to track every detail in a repository's history, making the project's evolution path transparent and accessible. Searching for a certain line in a project, finding its history and relevant details about it helps us to easily find the origin of every behaviour seen in a program. It also highlights the importance of committing practice. By keeping commits short and specific, we can enhance the maintainability and robustness of a project and facilitate the debugging process.
